## Headline

CardHouse – Your Online Card Room for Chaotic, Cozy Game Nights

## Tagline

Jump into a room, grab a seat at a table, and let the cards (and chaos) fly.

## Short Description

CardHouse is a browser-based online card room built for real friends, real voices, and real-time chaos. Spin up a room in seconds, share a link or a Discord slash command, and suddenly you’ve got a full house: multiple tables of Bluff, Poker, and classic card games running side by side, with everyone drifting between them like a real casino that forgot to take itself too seriously.

No installs, no accounts, no friction. Just a deck, a room full of people, and the kind of laughter you usually only get around a real table at 2 a.m.

## Long Description

CardHouse is what happens when you take the energy of a late-night home game and wire it directly into the browser.

You start with a **room** – a shared lobby that can hold your whole crew. From there, anyone can spin up a table: Bluff here, Poker there, a trick-taking table in the corner. Some people dive straight into the action. Others lounge in the lobby, watch the tables, trash-talk in chat, or wait for a seat to open up. It feels less like joining a “match” and more like stepping into a familiar hangout spot.

Under the hood, CardHouse is real-time, low-friction, and ruthlessly simple on purpose. On the surface, it’s pixels, cards, chips, and voices. The experience is stripped down to the things that actually matter when you’re playing with people you know: clear rules, readable cards, fast rounds, and just enough UI to get out of the way once the jokes start.

Whether you’re running game night in a Discord server or just sending a link in the group chat, CardHouse is built to be the easiest way to say: “Let’s just play.”

## Key Features

**Host a whole card room, not just one match**  

Create a single lobby and let up to dozens of people wander in. From there, anyone can open a table: multiple Bluff tables, multiple Poker tables, and other classics all running in parallel.

**Instant join, no accounts**  

Share a link, pick a name, and you’re in. No signups, no logins, no friction. It’s closer to opening your front door than onboarding into yet another platform.

**Tight Discord integration**  

Drop a slash command in your Discord channel to spawn a fresh CardHouse room. The bot spins up the lobby, creates per-table voice channels, and maps Discord usernames into the game. When someone speaks in voice, their in-game avatar lights up – you see the conversation without ever touching their audio.

**Jump between tables mid-session**  

Play a few hands of Poker, stand up, and slide into a Bluff table across the room. Tables are lightweight and disposable; the room is the constant. If there’s an empty seat, it’s yours.

**Crisp pixel-art card tables**  

Every game shares the same lovingly crafted pixel-art deck, chips, and table. It keeps the visuals clean and familiar, so you can focus on reading people, not reading the UI.

**Built for real-time banter**  

Lobby chat, table chat, voice indicators, and fast state updates make CardHouse feel alive even when half the room is just spectating and stirring the pot.

## Ideal Use Cases

- Weekly online game nights where everyone drifts in and out over a few hours.

- Discord communities that want a “card room” vibe running alongside their main chat.

- Friends who are tired of heavyweight poker apps and just want something lighter, faster, and more social.

- Remote teams looking for a low-friction social break that feels more like a living room than a leaderboard.

## Tone & Positioning

CardHouse isn’t trying to be a hyper-serious casino simulator. It’s built for **human, messy, funny** nights where someone misplays, someone misclicks, and everyone remembers the story more than the score. The tech is there to make sure everything is smooth and fair. The point is everything that happens around the table.

Open a room, drop the link, and see who sits down first. The cards are ready.
