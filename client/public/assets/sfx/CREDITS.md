# Walnut Club sound credits

Recordings from Freesound were trimmed, equalized, mixed to mono, and leveled. Some cues combine multiple edits. The pitched user interface cues were designed with [jsfxr 1.4.1](https://sfxr.me/) and layered with damped harmonics.
