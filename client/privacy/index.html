Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy covers the Cardhouse web game and Discord bot, operated by Studio Arkaive. It explains what information Cardhouse processes, who can access it, and how to request a copy, correction, or deletion of your information.

Information we process

  • Discord commands and profiles. Discord sends interaction data when you use a Cardhouse slash command. We process the command and its options, room codes, and the server and channel identifiers needed to operate the bot. When you join with Discord information, we process your Discord user ID and any username, display name, or legacy discriminator supplied with the join. We may look up your Discord profile to suggest a player name.
  • Optional Discord account linking. If you choose to link your account, we use Discord authorization to verify your user ID and associate it with your Cardhouse session identity. We use a temporary cookie that expires after five minutes to verify the authorization request. A linked account can save an avatar and use Discord voice features in supported rooms.
  • Voice channel activity. For Cardhouse voice channels, we process Discord user and channel identifiers and voice presence or speaking status when available. Discord handles the audio. Cardhouse uses presence metadata for in-game indicators and to move linked players already in Discord voice between the room and its tables.
  • Game activity and chat. We store player names, room and table membership, game moves and results, connection status, avatar appearance and movement, virtual chip activity, and chat messages you send in Cardhouse. Where Discord chat mirroring is enabled, we also process messages, display names, user and message IDs, and edits or deletions from the linked room or table channel.
  • Browser storage and diagnostics. Cardhouse stores a player ID, database session token, saved room and table information, player names, avatar and appearance settings, and audio preferences in browser local storage. Our services and infrastructure providers may process network and diagnostic information, such as IP addresses, request times, and connection or error events, to deliver and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information to create, join, run, and close rooms and tables, deliver game state and chat, manage Discord voice channels, show who is in voice, link accounts when requested, restore sessions and preferences, maintain virtual chip balances and leaderboards, investigate problems, and respond to support and privacy requests.

Who can see information

The bot posts room and command information and room leaderboards in Discord. Players and spectators can see player names, game activity, presence indicators, and chat in the relevant Cardhouse screens. When mirroring is enabled, messages from linked Discord channels are visible in the corresponding Cardhouse room or table chat. The game also uses publicly readable database tables. People who connect to the database may be able to read records outside their current room, including room and table data, player names, Discord user and channel identifiers, Discord profile information, voice presence, virtual chip balances, and in-game chat. Keep sensitive information out of player names and chat.

We exchange interaction, profile, and voice channel information with Discord to operate the integration. We use Hostinger for hosting and SpacetimeDB for the multiplayer database. These providers process information needed for the services they provide. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. Discord separately handles information you provide through its platform. Discord Privacy Policy

How long we keep information

The production game starts closing a room after an hour of inactivity. Room cleanup removes membership, chat, voice presence, and room wallets from the live database. When the bot still needs to remove a Discord voice channel, the closed room can remain until it finishes. Table cleanup removes associated messages and events when it deletes a table. Archived chip totals, Discord account links, and saved linked avatars can remain after room cleanup. Browser storage remains on your device until the game removes those items or you clear the site's data.

Your choices and requests

You can change your display name in the game and clear your site data in your browser settings. Clearing site data resets local settings and reconnect information. To ask us to access, correct, or delete information, including Discord data or a stored account link, email contact@arkaive.studio. Share enough detail to identify the records, such as your player name, a relevant room code, or your Discord user ID. Keep your passwords and session tokens private.

We may ask you to verify your request before acting. We handle verified requests in accordance with applicable law and Discord's requirements. Where applicable, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, request a portable copy, or complain to your local data protection authority.

Age requirements and updates

Cardhouse is for users aged 13 or older who also meet any higher minimum age that applies in their country. If you believe a child below the required age has shared information with us, use the contact above to request removal. When we update this policy, we will post the new version and its effective date here.
